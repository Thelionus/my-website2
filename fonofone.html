Fonofone Publications  |  Instrument  |  Educational Project

“Listening is a learned, creative act — Fonofone gives young people the tools to discover that for themselves.”

Fonofone (also known as Musicolateur) is a sound-creation instrument co-invented by Yves Daoust and Alex Burton in 2004 to awaken musical creativity in young people. Designed to be intuitive yet musically rich, it has become the cornerstone of a sustained pedagogical engagement reaching thousands of students across Québec and beyond.
